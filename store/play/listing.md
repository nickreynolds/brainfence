## Full description (4,000 characters max)

> Brainfence keeps your distracting apps and websites blocked until you've done what you meant to do today.
>
> Set up your tasks and habits (a morning walk, a meditation, a journal entry, a routine, a shopping trip), then make blocking rules: which apps and sites stay blocked, and which tasks unlock them. Finish the tasks and they open up again.
>
> BUILT SO YOU CAN'T TALK YOURSELF OUT OF IT
> • Loosening a rule is time-locked, so you can't switch it off on a whim.
> • Tasks can check themselves: GPS tasks complete when you arrive or leave, timed tasks when the timer runs out, and companion-app tasks when you've spent the time in an app such as your meditation app.
> • Blocking works in Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Brave, Samsung Internet and other supported browsers, and covers browsers it can't check while sites are blocked.
>
> MORE THAN A BLOCKER
> • Routines with steps, recurring tasks and time windows
> • Shopping lists that remind you when you're near the store
> • Journal, contemplations delivered through the day, and reports to track how you're doing
> • A setup guide with starter tasks to get going in a few minutes
>
> EVERYWHERE YOU WORK
> Brainfence also runs on Windows and Mac. Your tasks and rules sync between your devices and keep working offline.
>
> PRIVACY
> Brainfence uses Android's Accessibility Service to see which app is open and, in supported browsers, which website you're on, so it can block them. That stays on your phone: it isn't saved or sent anywhere. No ads, no analytics, no selling your data. Details: https://brainfence.online/privacy/

Before publishing, check the browser list against
`android/app/src/main/java/dev/brainfence/service/WebsiteBlocker.kt`
(`ADDRESS_BAR_IDS`).
