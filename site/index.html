Brainfence

Protect your head. Brainfence blocks your distracting apps and websites until your tasks and habits for the day are done.

Coming soon to Android, Windows and Mac

How it works

  • Set up tasks and routines: a morning walk, meditation, a journal entry, anything with a checkbox.
  • Make blocking rules: which apps and sites stay blocked, and which tasks unlock them.
  • Loosening a rule takes time, so you can't undo it on a whim.
  • Your tasks and rules sync between your phone and computers, and work offline.
